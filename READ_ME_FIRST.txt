APMHSS PORTABLE TIMETABLE

1. Open START_APMHSS.bat or APMHSS_Time_Table_Updated.exe.
2. All new edits are saved automatically to SQLite files inside this folder.
3. Close the app before copying the folder to your pen drive.
4. Copy the ENTIRE PORTABLE_APP folder to another Windows computer.
5. Never copy only the EXE, or your saved data will not travel.
6. The database files are required; do not delete them.
7. Back up the entire folder regularly.
8. This is OFFLINE portability, not simultaneous synchronization.
